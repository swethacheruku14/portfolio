About

I work as a System Engineer at Taashee Linux Services Private Limited on digital infrastructure. The day-to-day work is designing, troubleshooting, and maintaining networks in LAN and WAN environments, and staying with a problem until the path, the device, or the carrier is actually fixed.

Most of that time is on enterprise routers and switches: Cisco ASR 1001-X, ISR 4331-X, and C7200; Catalyst C9200, C9300, C3850, and C2960; Juniper EX4200, MX240, and MX480; plus Aruba. I also run pre-deployment checks — building the topology on a test bed so a live cutover is not the first time the design is proven.

I am comfortable on incident bridges with global engineering teams, and I write the outcome down: health checks, RCA, and what changed. Network automation is the next layer on that same work, and this site has a section ready for those write-ups.
